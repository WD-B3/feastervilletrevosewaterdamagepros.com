Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main water shutoff before anything else.

Cut power safely

Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel on a dry floor.

Lift what you can

Hatboro conditions that matter

Pennypack Creek flows through the middle of the borough, so check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurance agent about flood coverage. Flood damage is usually not covered by a standard homeowners policy. The Upper Moreland-Hatboro Joint Sewer Authority provides sewage service to Hatboro and neighboring Upper Moreland Township.

Because Hatboro is in Montgomery County, permits and inspections for repairs go through the borough rather than Lower Southampton Township. Ask the borough building department what applies to your project.

Common causes of water damage in Hatboro homes

Any home in the borough can face the same short list of problems. Supply lines and water heaters fail, washing machine hoses split, roofs leak after storms and basements take on water when the ground is saturated. Each cause has its own page, and the right one depends on where the water came from.

  • Burst or frozen pipes, covered on the burst pipe page and frozen pipe page.
  • Appliance and water heater failures, covered on the appliance leak page and water heater page.
  • Wind-driven rain and roof leaks, covered on the storm damage page.
  • Wet basements, covered on the basement page.

If you are not sure which applies, describe the water to dispatch and they will point you to the right work.

What happens when you call from Hatboro

Dispatch asks for your address, the source of the water and what it has touched. If the water is still running or near electrical equipment, you will get safety steps first. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.

On site, the crew reads moisture, places extraction and drying equipment and records what it finds. The water extraction page and the structural drying page explain each phase. Readings are repeated until the structure matches the dry standard for your home.

Insurance questions in Hatboro

Coverage depends on the cause of the water and the terms of your policy. Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer, because only the insurer can decide.

We document the loss with photos, moisture readings and a written scope that you can pass along. The Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises homeowners to photograph damage before cleanup and to keep every receipt, and that advice applies in Montgomery County just as it does in Bucks County.
