Emergency water heater leak cleanup in Feasterville Trevose, PA deals with water released by a failed tank or a leaking connection. A tank heater holds around 40 or more gallons, and when the tank fails it can empty onto the floor within minutes. Water usually pools in basements or utility rooms.

This page covers the heater itself. Water on a basement floor is covered more broadly on the basement page, and a pipe elsewhere in the house is on the burst pipe page.

When a water heater leak is an emergency

A leaking tank is an emergency if water is spreading. Shut off the water to the heater and call the 24/7 line. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Water heater safety

  1. Shut off the cold water supply valve on the heater.
  2. Turn off the power at the breaker for an electric heater, or the gas control for a gas heater, only if you can do so safely.
  3. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911.
  4. Do not stand in water near the electrical panel or the heater wiring.
  5. Photograph the heater, the label and the water.

Why tanks fail

Age and corrosion

Tank liners wear out over time and rust through.

Sediment buildup

Mineral deposits stress the bottom of the tank.

Pressure relief valve discharge

The valve opens when pressure or temperature is too high.

Loose or corroded connections

Fittings leak at supply and drain points.

A small drip from a fitting can be fixed by a plumber. A leak from the tank body means replacement. Your water utility publishes water quality information, and hard water can speed up sediment buildup, so ask your utility about yours.

What the water reaches

Heaters often sit in basements near laundry areas, so water can reach finished walls, stored boxes and carpet. Water from a tank is clean when it starts, but it picks up dirt from the floor. Drywall bottoms and insulation wick it upward.

A moisture meter and thermal imaging check the walls and floor around the heater. Stored items on the floor should be moved quickly, because cardboard and paper absorb water and invite mold.

Cleanup and drying

The tank water is extracted with pumps or an extraction unit. Wet materials that cannot dry are removed, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry the area. Readings are logged. The plumber replaces the heater, and we coordinate so the floor is dry before the new unit is set.

Floor drains in basements can back up if they are overwhelmed, so confirm the water is going away. For general drying methods see structural drying.

Standards for tank failures

Water from a failed tank is usually clean supply water, Category 1 under ANSI/IICRC S500, though sediment and time can matter. Ask the crew which category it assigned and why.

Safety steps for a leaking water heater

A leaking tank can involve hot water and live power or gas. Treat it with care.

  1. Turn off the power or gas to the heater if you can reach the controls safely.
  2. Close the cold water supply valve to the tank.
  3. Keep clear of very hot water.
  4. Call your utility or 911 for any gas smell.

Replacement and prevention

Tank heaters wear out. Many fail with little warning, so signs such as rust-colored water, a rumbling tank or moisture at the base deserve a plumber visit. Putting the heater in a drain pan with a line to a floor drain limits damage from a leak.

  1. Know where the cold water shutoff to the heater is.
  2. Look at the base of the tank for rust or moisture a few times a year.
  3. Ask a plumber about the age of your tank.
  4. Move stored boxes off the floor around the heater.
