Emergency frozen pipe water damage restoration in Feasterville Trevose, PA covers the flooding that happens when a frozen pipe splits and the ice thaws. The water often does not show until the pipe warms, which can be hours or days after the freeze.

This page is about freeze damage. A pipe that burst without freezing is covered on the burst pipe page. Winter in Bucks County can bring hard freezes, so ask your plumber or check National Weather Service records for local freeze information. We do not quote a local frequency here.

When a frozen pipe is an emergency

A frozen pipe becomes an emergency when it thaws and leaks. Call the 24/7 line at any hour. Same-day response is offered when crews are available. If you suspect a pipe froze but nothing leaks yet, shut off the main water to limit the risk.

Safe steps after a freeze

  1. Close the main water valve if a pipe is frozen or has split.
  2. Open the affected faucet and let it drip while the pipe thaws, once the main is off.
  3. Do not use an open flame or a torch on a frozen pipe.
  4. If water is near outlets or the panel, call your utility or 911 about any electrical risk.
  5. Photograph the split and the damage.

Where pipes freeze

  • Pipes in exterior walls with poor insulation
  • Pipes in unheated spaces such as crawl spaces, garages and attics
  • Lines near vents, foundation gaps or cold windows
  • Outdoor hose bibs and sprinkler lines
  • Pipes in vacant or seasonal homes

The split often forms where ice has expanded, not where the ice began. A pipe can burst a few feet from the frozen section.

The thaw problem

Frozen pipes do not leak while frozen. A split may go unnoticed until the weather warms or the heat comes back, at which point water runs freely. If you are away when it thaws, the water can run for hours.

This is why checking pipes after a cold snap matters. A moisture meter and thermal camera find water that is running inside walls and floors. Wet drywall and insulation are removed where needed, and the cavity is dried.

Cleanup and repair

Extraction comes first, followed by drying. The plumber repairs the pipe. Once readings are normal, drywall, insulation and flooring are repaired. The structural drying page explains how readings are used.

Pipes in a crawl space and in exterior walls are common freeze points, and each has its own damage pattern. Insulating exposed pipes and keeping heat on in unoccupied rooms reduce the risk.

Standards and thawed-pipe water

Once a frozen line thaws and leaks, the water is usually clean supply water, which ANSI/IICRC S500 calls Category 1. The published standard still calls for extraction, moisture mapping and drying to a target before the walls are closed.

Preventing freeze damage next winter

  1. Insulate pipes in crawl spaces, garages, attics and exterior walls.
  2. Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor spigots before cold weather.
  3. Keep the heat on in unoccupied rooms and open cabinet doors on exterior walls.
  4. Let a faucet drip during a hard freeze to keep water moving.
  5. If you travel in winter, set the heat to a safe minimum and ask someone to check the house.

A plumber can tell you which pipes in your house are most exposed, and your insurer can tell you what the policy requires for winter vacancy.

Questions about frozen pipe water damage in Feasterville Trevose

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Feasterville Trevose?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when crews are available. Shut off the main water valve and photograph the damage while you wait.

Why did my pipe not leak until days later?

Ice blocks the split. When it thaws, water flows. That can happen days after the freeze.

Can I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Gently warming with a hair dryer on a pipe with the faucet open can work, but never use a flame. If the pipe is split, shut off the main and call a plumber.

Will insurance cover freeze damage?

Often, if it is sudden and the home was properly heated, but policies differ. Ask your insurer.
