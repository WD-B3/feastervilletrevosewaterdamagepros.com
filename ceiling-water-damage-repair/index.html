Emergency ceiling water damage repair in Feasterville Trevose, PA starts with finding where the water came from. A ceiling stain is only the end of the path. The water may have come through roof decking, from an upstairs bathroom, along a supply line or from an HVAC unit in the attic.

This page also covers roof leaks, because a roof leak shows up as a wet ceiling and is repaired the same way inside. The roofing itself is a separate trade. For an appliance or a pipe as the source, see appliance leak water damage.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

A ceiling that is dripping, bulging or sagging is an emergency. Water pooled above drywall adds weight fast, and a sagging ceiling can fall. Call the 24/7 line at any hour. Same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Safe steps while you wait

  1. Stay out from under a bulging or sagging ceiling and keep children and pets away.
  2. Place a bucket under active drips and move furniture aside.
  3. If water is near a light fixture or fan, leave the switch off and call your utility or 911 about any electrical risk.
  4. If the source is a pipe above, shut off the water to that fixture or the main valve.
  5. Photograph the stain, the drip and any sag.

Some homeowners relieve a bulge by poking a small hole and catching the water in a bucket. That can limit a collapse, but only when you are standing clear of the area that might fall.

Reading the stain

A brown ring with a darker edge usually means water has come through more than once. A fresh gray stain with a wet center is more recent. A stain that spreads after a rain points to the roof. A stain that spreads when someone uses a bathroom upstairs points to plumbing.

Roof leak

Staining follows storms, often near a wall, chimney or vent.

Bathroom above

Staining appears when a shower, tub or toilet is used upstairs.

Supply line

Steady staining that does not depend on weather or fixture use.

HVAC condensate

Staining under an attic air handler, often in warm months.

The AC leak page covers condensate drain lines in detail.

Roof leaks and the ceiling below

Roof water travels. It enters at a flashing, a damaged shingle or a vent boot, then runs along rafters and insulation before it drips through drywall far from the entry. Wet attic insulation holds water against the ceiling above the room and keeps it wet.

A restorer removes the wet ceiling and insulation, dries the framing and puts up containment if mold is a concern. A roofer repairs the entry point. Without that repair the ceiling will stain again. We will say so plainly when a roof repair has to come first.

How the ceiling is dried and repaired

A moisture meter and thermal imaging camera show how far the water spread above the ceiling. Wet drywall that is sagging, soft or stained with active leaks is cut out. Wet insulation is removed and the joists are dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Repair then follows drying. New drywall is hung and finished, stain-blocking primer is applied and the ceiling is painted. Painting over a stain without drying first often traps moisture and the stain returns. The drying process is explained on the structural drying page.

The standard behind ceiling work

ANSI/IICRC S500 treats the source and the age of the water as the starting point. A clean roof or pipe leak is handled differently from water that has picked up contamination in an attic or wall cavity.

Safety steps for a wet ceiling

A ceiling that holds water can fall without warning, and ceiling fixtures can carry water into wiring. Stay out from under a bulging ceiling.

  1. Move people and pets out of the room below.
  2. Turn off the light circuit if you can reach the panel safely.
  3. Place a bucket under any drip.
  4. Do not poke a bulge unless you can do so from a safe position and have a way to catch the water.

Questions about ceiling water damage repair in Feasterville Trevose

Do you handle ceiling water emergencies at night or on weekends in Feasterville Trevose?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when crews are available. Stay out from under a sagging ceiling and shut off any plumbing source you can reach safely.

Can I just paint over a ceiling stain?

Not until the ceiling and framing are dry and the source is fixed. Paint over a damp ceiling can trap moisture and the stain bleeds through again.

Do you repair the roof?

Roof repair is a separate trade. We dry and repair the interior and will tell you when a roofer has to fix the entry point first.

How do I know whether the leak is from the roof or plumbing?

Timing helps. A leak that appears after rain points to the roof. A leak that appears when an upstairs fixture runs points to plumbing. A moisture meter and thermal imaging confirm the path.
