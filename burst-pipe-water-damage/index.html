Emergency burst pipe water damage cleanup in Feasterville Trevose, PA begins the moment the water is shut off. A pressurized supply line can release hundreds of gallons in an hour, and the water spreads through ceilings, walls and floors before it is found.

This page covers a pipe that burst or broke under pressure. Pipes that freeze have their own page, frozen pipe water damage, and a leak from a tank is covered at water heater leak cleanup.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

A burst pipe is an emergency every time. Shut off the main water valve first, then call the 24/7 line. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, including nights and weekends.

What to do in the first minutes

  1. Close the main shutoff valve and open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the line.
  2. If water is near the panel or outlets, stay out and call your utility or 911 about any electrical risk.
  3. Move valuables and furniture off wet floors.
  4. Do not use ceilings that are bulging or dripping.
  5. Photograph the broken pipe and the damage before cleanup.

Know where the main valve is before you need it. If you cannot find it, the water meter has a shutoff, and your utility can help.

Why pipes burst

Age and corrosion

Older copper and galvanized pipe can fail at joints and thin spots.

High water pressure

Pressure above what fixtures are built for strains connections.

Freezing

Ice expands inside pipe and splits it, often in an exterior wall or an unheated space.

Poor repairs

Fittings and solder joints can fail years after installation.

A pinhole leak in copper can look small, but it can run for weeks behind a wall. Burst pipes in ceilings often show up as wet drywall that suddenly sags.

How the cleanup runs

Extraction removes standing water. A moisture meter and thermal camera follow the water through floors, walls and ceilings. Wet drywall and insulation that cannot dry in place are cut out, and the crew opens enough to reach the wet cavity.

Supply water starts as clean water, but it can degrade if it sits. Drying equipment is placed quickly, and readings are logged. The pipe itself is repaired by a plumber. We coordinate timing so drying is not started while water still runs.

Water that fell through floors can wet rooms below. The ceiling page covers the repair of drywall from above, and structural drying covers the equipment.

Standards behind the drying plan

A fresh supply line break is Category 1 water under ANSI/IICRC S500, but the standard notes that water can move to a higher category as it sits. That is why published practice favors fast extraction and a written drying plan.

Know where your main shutoff is

Most homes have a main water shutoff near where the line enters the house, often in a basement, utility room or garage. A water meter near the street also has a shutoff, but a utility key may be needed. Find the valve now and test it so it turns.

  1. Locate the valve and tag it so family members can find it.
  2. Turn it off and on once a year to keep it from seizing.
  3. Check for slow drips at valves and joints near the main.
  4. Take a photo of the valve for quick reference during an emergency.
