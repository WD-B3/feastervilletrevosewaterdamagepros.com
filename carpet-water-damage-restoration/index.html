Carpet water damage restoration in Feasterville Trevose, PA means deciding fast whether carpet and pad can be saved. Carpet fibers dry quickly, but the pad underneath holds water and the subfloor below it stays wet. Whether you keep the carpet comes down to the water source and how long it sat.

This page covers carpet. Wood and laminate floors behave differently, so see hardwood floor water damage repair. If the whole basement flooded, the basement page covers the wider job.

Save it or replace it

Restorers sort carpet by water category and time. Clean supply water that is extracted within a day or so often leaves carpet that can be dried in place. Water from a washer discharge or a dirty source calls for more caution. Floodwater or sewage means the carpet and pad are removed.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard guides these calls, and your restorer should explain each decision.

Signs your carpet is still wet

  • A squishy feel under your feet, even if the surface looks dry
  • A musty odor, especially in a closed room
  • Carpet that is slightly raised or rippled
  • Dark spots or discoloration along walls and edges
  • A cold, damp feeling in the room after the source was fixed

Odor is worth acting on. A musty smell means moisture is still present somewhere. Callers often notice it after pulling up a carpet corner and finding a bit of mold. If that happens, see the mold remediation page.

How wet carpet is dried

Extraction first removes water from the carpet and pad with a weighted wand. If the carpet is saved, it is lifted at the edges and floated on air so air movers can dry both sides. Dehumidifiers dry the room. The pad is removed or replaced when it cannot be cleaned.

Moisture meters read the subfloor and wall bottoms, because water often wicks into baseboards and drywall. Crews also check the tack strip and the edge of the carpet. Readings are taken each day until they return to normal.

Carpet cleaning after drying removes odor and residue. That is the last step, not the first.

What to do before the crew arrives

You can improve the odds of saving carpet in the first hour. Stop the water, then lift furniture legs off the wet carpet and place foil or plastic under any legs that must stay. Wood and metal legs leave stains that are hard to remove once wet.

  1. Remove small rugs, boxes and paper from the wet area.
  2. Open closet doors and lift hanging items so hems stay dry.
  3. Run the air conditioning or a fan on the room only if the power is safe.
  4. Do not walk on soaked carpet more than necessary, because it spreads water and dirt.

Keep a piece of the carpet and a label from the pad if the carpet is cut out. Your insurer may ask for the type and age of the floor covering.

Questions about carpet water damage in Feasterville Trevose

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, when the water was clean and extraction started promptly. Contaminated water or several days of wetness usually means replacement.

Why does pad matter so much?

Pad absorbs and holds water long after the carpet surface feels dry. It also traps odor and mold below the carpet.

How long should I wait before I call?

Call as soon as you find the water. Wet carpet can begin to smell musty in a short time, and drying in place only works when the work starts early.

Will cleaning the carpet fix water damage?

Cleaning removes dirt and odor after the carpet and subfloor are dry. It does not dry the pad or remove trapped moisture.
