Water damage restoration in Feasterville Trevose, PA is one job with many starting points. A page for each source, material or space means you land on steps written for your exact problem. The groups below follow the cause, and the homepage explains the whole process for water damage restoration as a service.

Pipes and plumbing

When water comes from a line under pressure, start with burst pipe water damage, or frozen pipe damage if a freeze split the pipe. A tank failure is covered at water heater leak cleanup, and a hidden line under a floor at slab leak water damage.

Appliances and HVAC

A washer, dishwasher or refrigerator failure is on the appliance leak page. Cabinets and the sink area are covered at kitchen water damage, and condensate problems at AC leak water damage.

Weather and building envelope

Creek and groundwater flooding is on the flood damage page, and wind and rain damage on storm water damage. Rooms and spaces have their own pages too, including ceilings and roof leaks, basements and crawl spaces.

Water removal and drying

Standing water starts with water extraction. The drying that follows is explained at structural drying and dehumidification, and contaminated water at sewage backup cleanup.

Floors and walls

Wood and laminate floors are on the hardwood floor page, soaked carpet on carpet water damage, and stained or soft walls on wall and drywall repair.

Commercial and secondary services

Stores, offices and multi-unit buildings are on the commercial water damage page. After a leak that stayed wet, the mold remediation page covers containment, removal and clearance testing, our secondary service.

Which page should I start with?

If you are unsure, start with the source of the water. Water from a pipe, an appliance, the roof or the ground each have a page. If the water is already standing, call the 24/7 line at (445) 837-0074 and describe it. Dispatch will point you to the right work.
