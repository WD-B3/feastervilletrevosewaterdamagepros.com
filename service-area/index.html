The Feasterville Trevose area and its water

Feasterville and Trevose sit in the Neshaminy Creek watershed. The National Weather Service tracks the creek at a gauge near Langhorne, with a flood stage of 9 feet and a record crest of 22.84 feet in 1955. Homes near the water should check the FEMA flood map for their address.

Lower Southampton Township reports that groundwater and surface water enter its sewer system during heavy rain, and that its flow is treated at the Philadelphia Water Department's Northeast Wastewater Treatment Plant. Low points in a home, such as basements, are where overloaded drains show up first.

Hatboro and Montgomery County

Hatboro is a borough of about 8,200 people on roughly 1.4 square miles. Pennypack Creek runs through its center, and the Upper Moreland-Hatboro Joint Sewer Authority provides sewage service to the borough and neighboring Upper Moreland Township.

Because Hatboro is in Montgomery County, permits and inspections run through the borough and not through Lower Southampton Township. Ask the borough building department what applies to your repair.
